About San Ramon General Trading & Cont. Co.

San Ramon General Trading & Cont. Co. is a trademark in Kuwait which offers high quality products and services locally and globally. San Ramon’s competitive advantage lies in its wide distribution network. In 2004, San Ramon established its first department, producing products under the gas and oil sector. A few years later in 2012, San Ramon expanded to include food and beverages with a focus on production and distribution. San Ramon is the owner and main manufacturer of many brands under this department, namely Karak Tea, Shahad Honey, Food Hacks, and Food Experts. A year later in 2013, San Ramon added further diversity by offering beauty products, and in 2015, San Ramon opened it fourth department, cafes and restaurants to offer services. Soon enough in 2018, San Ramon established its fifth department under interior designing, and aspires to have more departments and collaborations in the near future.
