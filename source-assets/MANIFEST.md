Every file below was downloaded from the live WordPress site https://sanramonkw.com on 2026-07-02.
Format: `local path | original URL`.

Notes:
- Client logos (2024/03 uploads, .webp) keep their original bilingual filenames here; ASCII-renamed copies live in `public/images/clients/` for URL safety.

- `branding/logo-w.jpg` is both the header logo and the original favicon; `branding/sanramon-logo.png` is the large footer wordmark.
